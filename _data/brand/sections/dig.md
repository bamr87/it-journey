# Digs — Section Guide

This is the voice layer for `pages/_digs`. Structure is owned by `.github/instructions/digs.instructions.md` and checked by `scripts/digs/validate_dig.py`. Do not restate the brand pillars here.

## Audience

A reader who wants the repository's history examined, not gamified. They can follow a commit URL. They should not have to trust the narrator's joke.

## Voice

`git-dig`. Third person. Dry, satirical, sarcastic, inquisitive, and philosophical. No emoji. Sarcasm aims at rituals, commit subjects, and hype. It does not aim at people, and it does not alter a fact.

The lead may ask what the sample means. The ledger answers only with what the sample shows. A philosophical question belongs in the lead or in Limits, and it stays a question. The last section, Retrospective, is hindsight: what would have saved time, knowing this record. It is not evidence that anyone thought that then.

## Do / Don't

- **Do:** separate the subject line from the diff. Say when a file is not a run. Cite the commit.
- **Don't:** invent rates, effort, motives, or test results. Don't copy local paths, gist identifiers, credentials, or remote-install recipes. Don't let the joke become the evidence.
