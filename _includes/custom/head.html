{%- comment -%} custom/head.html — this site's addition to the theme's . The remote theme (bamr87/zer0-mistakes) includes _includes/custom/head.html from core/head.html as an empty stub, so this file is the sanctioned way to add markup site-wide without forking a theme file. It publishes the page's own identity for client-side scripts to read. The snippet clipper (assets/js/notes-clipper.js) needs to know what page a clip came from — its title, its URL, and which collection it belongs to — and reading it from meta tags keeps the clipper free of Liquid and free of any assumption about the theme's DOM. {%- endcomment -%}
